Concept 01

The Verdict Dial

One glance answers the only question that matters first: is this a deal, yes or no? The dial is a weighted composite of the individual pass/fail tests. Below it, each standard metric sits on its own track with its threshold marked as a notch — so you see not just the score, but exactly which test is dragging it down.

Concept 02

The Money Waterfall

Follow the dollars top to bottom. Each row is a real cash event — value in, costs out — and the bars physically shrink as money leaves the deal. You watch your profit or cash flow get carved out of the ARV, so an oversized rehab or a fat holding cost is impossible to miss. The last band is what you keep.

Concept 03

The Decision Card

A trading-card readout for a property — a single collectible object you can screenshot, text to a partner, or line up against three other deals. The letter grade is the composite; the stat block holds the standard numbers; the checklist translates each metric into plain-language pass/fail so a partner with no spreadsheet fluency still gets it.
